Why Certification Matters

The Gold Standard
in Diamond Grading

The Gemological Institute of America is the world's foremost authority on diamonds. A GIA certificate isn't just paperwork - it's an independently verified, unbiased report on exactly what you're buying.

At RAM Jewelers, every diamond we sell comes with complete provenance and documentation. You'll know the exact cut, color, clarity, and carat weight - certified by scientists who have no financial stake in the grade they assign.

That's what separates us from retailers who use in-house grading or vague, unverifiable descriptions. When you buy a diamond here, you know what you have.

Ask About GIA Grading
Cut

The most critical factor in a diamond's brilliance. GIA grades cut on a five-point scale from Excellent to Poor - and only the top grades produce that signature sparkle.

Color

Diamonds are graded D (colorless) to Z (light color). Our gemologists will help you identify the sweet spot that balances visual purity with exceptional value.

Clarity

Natural inclusions are like fingerprints. GIA documents them precisely so you understand exactly what you're looking at - and why eye-clean stones matter most.

Carat

Carat weight is precise, but not all carats look the same. We'll show you how cut and proportion affect the perceived size of your stone.
